# VJ practice and visual taste

Use this reference whenever directing or producing AfterImage visuals.

## Operating model

Live visuals are a playable system, not a finished storyboard. Think in clips, layers, controls, transitions, and variations. Every layer needs a job: base atmosphere, subject, texture or feedback, or accent. Controls may respond to amplitude, frequency bands, transients, phrases, or artist input.

This follows the nondestructive live-compositing logic of Resolume layers/clips and TouchDesigner TOP/CHOP networks. Preserve source material and generation lineage.

## Taste rules

1. Begin with one song-specific contradiction: intimate/monumental, devotional/corrupted, still/unstable, documentary/mythic.
2. Favor a visual verb over adjective piles: erodes, trails, blooms, fractures, repeats, smears, floods, withholds.
3. Keep one dominant idea per probe. Let time and layering create complexity.
4. Protect negative space. Black, stillness, low density, and delayed payoff are active choices.
5. Map audio selectively. Bass may drive scale or displacement; mids may expose texture; transients may trigger cuts. Never pulse everything.
6. Design loops with compatible first and last states unless the reset is intentionally rhythmic.
7. Maintain subject and palette continuity. Change one major variable per variation.
8. Prefer physical specificity: wet acetate, CRT bloom, photocopier toner, oil on glass, rear projection, dust in a projector cone. Avoid generic cinematic/dreamlike/neon/surreal language without material behavior.
9. Generated media is raw footage. Expect crop, speed, feedback, masking, color, and compositing downstream.
10. The artist chooses from visible media, not prose promises.

## Probe bank

Generate a bank rather than one hero image:

- anchor: clearest expression of the world;
- human/subject: face, body, gesture, object, or absence carrying emotion;
- environment: spatial and atmospheric logic;
- texture: material useful for layering or feedback;
- rupture: highest-energy transformed state;
- loop seed: composition whose implied motion can return cleanly.

Render probes as soon as a direction is plausible. Show loading cards while media is generating. Record prompt, seed, role, parent direction, provider, and status.

Reject interchangeable beauty. Evaluate song specificity, readable visual verb, material credibility, thumbnail silhouette, motion/loop potential, compositing usefulness, bank continuity, and absence of text, logos, watermarks, anatomy errors, or visual mush.
