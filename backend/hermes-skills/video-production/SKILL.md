# Video Production

Translate the approved treatment into one coherent hero-frame prompt suitable for a cinematic 16:9 music-video still. Specify subject, environment, lighting, lens, composition, motion implication, texture, and exclusions. Preserve continuity and avoid text, logos, watermarks, and unsafe imagery.
